Muddle
Lore:
Humans thrived on Earth for far too many years, eventually settling into stagnancy. With every plot of land owned, it became legally impossible to mine for stone. So they made the Quarry, on the Moon. This made the moon lighter and the earth heavier with each passing day. Until eventually, the Moon was dragged down to Earth, and crashed. Though too light at that point to cause absolute death, it flooded the world and brought a sixth mass extinction. Only a few of Animalia survived. The most significant being the rodents, the bugs, and the fish. Yes the bats survived too because a bat cave in a game is awesome. But the Drones, too, survived. Humans made life that would outlast themselves, the rodents are wise, having been passed down generations of personal knowledge from their masters, their owners. They're smart, can speak, can make civilization. The Drones are vastly more intelligent than rodents, but have a combined wisdom of 0. The only choice they made on their own is to take rodents' bodies to avoid the flood. (Yes this is sort of an alternate future, humans didnt invest so much into AI and rodents talk) They sent a research Drone, you, to find the perfect host in the land below the sea.
Gameplay:
It is entirely subterranean, above is the Flood, below is the water table. So everywhere is saturated mud, maybe some stone. Biomes are Mud, Batcave, Mangrove, River, maybe some more. You pretty much need to survive for a long time in certain races' bodies to find their weaknesses and strengths, you can upgrade your proficiency in the between-menu. There's host-neutral upgrades like luminescence that are far more useful than host-specific, but to beat the game you need to get all of one host's upgrades. You're sent in a pod that always lands in the Mud, near the surface. The game is horizontal mostly, vertically its not too big. I'm working on the worldgen right now in Godot. When a host dies (lack of food (bugs) freshwater and health (not being wounded) it still has a healthbar that slowly goes down, unhealable. Rot. You need to escape a rotting body and get another host, which you can only do when they're off-guard (sleeping or knocked out) killing one lets you take their body but it is decaying. There's no inventory. You're replaced every time you die, thats why dying lets you upgrade, whole new research drone. The world resets partially, the caves do, but progress in certain areas pre-built do not.
